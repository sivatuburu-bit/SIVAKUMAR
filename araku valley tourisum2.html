Welcome to Araku

Araku Valley is a beautiful hill station in Andhra Pradesh, surrounded by green mountains, valleys, waterfalls and coffee plantations.


It is also famous for its tribal culture, natural beauty, scenic viewpoints and pleasant atmosphere.

☕ Araku Coffee

Araku Valley is well known for its coffee plantations. The cool climate and mountain environment create excellent conditions for coffee cultivation.


Visitors can explore coffee plantations and learn about the journey from coffee beans to the final cup.

Travel Information

Useful information for tourists

🚆 By Train

Araku can be reached by train from Visakhapatnam. The railway journey is famous for its scenic mountain views.

🚗 By Road

Araku is connected by road with Visakhapatnam and other nearby areas.
