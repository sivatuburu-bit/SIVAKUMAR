The Destination

Where the river meets the hills

Papikondalu is best experienced from the Godavari itself.

GODAVARI RIVER
01

A landscape made for slow travel.

Papikondalu, or Papi Hills, forms part of the Eastern Ghats. The Godavari winds through the hills, creating a dramatic combination of river, forest and mountains.

The official Andhra Pradesh Tourism brochure highlights the cruise through Papi Hills as a scenic experience, with the river narrowing and passing through the hill range.

Before You Go

Travel smart

Simple things that make your journey more comfortable.

🌤️ Check the weather

River conditions and boat operations can vary. Check current conditions before travelling.

🎒 Carry essentials

Keep water, sun protection, comfortable clothing, a power bank and your camera/phone.

🚤 Follow boat instructions

Follow the operator's safety instructions and use the safety equipment provided as directed.

📅 Confirm your route

Boarding points, schedules and stops may vary. Confirm details with the operator before departure.
