Araku Valley Agricultural Farming

Agriculture is an important part of life in Araku Valley. The region's hills, forests, climate and fertile soils support different agricultural activities. Farmers cultivate crops using traditional knowledge along with modern farming practices.

☕ Coffee Farming

Araku Valley is well known for its coffee plantations. Coffee is grown in the valley's hilly environment under suitable shade and climatic conditions.

🌱 Traditional Farming

Local farmers have developed agricultural practices suited to the hilly landscape and local environment.

🌿 Organic Farming

Sustainable and nature-friendly farming practices can help protect soil, water and the surrounding ecosystem.

Araku Coffee Plantations

Coffee cultivation has become an important part of Araku Valley's agricultural landscape.

Coffee plantations are found across the hilly areas, where shade trees and the local climate provide suitable conditions for cultivation.

Araku coffee is also associated with the region's tribal farming communities and their agricultural traditions.

Farming Practices

🌳 Shade-Grown Farming

Shade trees can provide a suitable environment for coffee cultivation while supporting biodiversity within plantations.

💧 Water Management

Efficient use of available water is important for maintaining agricultural productivity, especially in hilly areas.

🌾 Crop Cultivation

Farmers cultivate different crops according to local conditions, soil, season and available resources.

♻️ Sustainable Agriculture

Sustainable farming practices can help maintain soil quality and protect the natural environment for future generations.
